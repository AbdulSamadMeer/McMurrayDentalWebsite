Clear Aligner Therapy

A Straighter Smile Without the Hardware

Invisalign® uses a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners to gradually shift your teeth into their ideal positions. Each set of aligners is worn for about two weeks, then replaced with the next set in the series — comfortably moving your teeth step by step toward a perfectly straight smile.

Because the aligners are removable, you can eat, drink, brush, and floss without any restrictions. And because they're virtually clear, most people won't even notice you're wearing them. Invisalign® is effective for teens and adults, and in many cases delivers results faster than traditional braces.

Invisalign® can correct

  • Crowded teeth
  • Widely spaced teeth and gaps
  • Overbite, underbite, and crossbite
  • Open bite
  • Mild to moderate misalignment
  • Relapse after previous orthodontic treatment

How Invisalign® Works

Four simple steps to a straighter, healthier smile.

1

3D Smile Scan

We take a digital scan of your teeth — no messy impressions — and show you a 3D preview of your results.

2

Custom Aligners Made

Your personalised series of clear aligners is precision-manufactured and shipped directly to our office.

3

Wear & Progress

Wear aligners 20–22 hours per day, swapping to the next set every two weeks. Check-ins every 6–8 weeks.

4

Retention

Once treatment is complete, we fit you with custom retainers to keep your new smile perfectly in place.
